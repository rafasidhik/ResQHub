Core Capabilities

Coordination built for the field

Rapid Rescue Dispatch

Rule-based triage and smart recommendation dispatch the right rescue units using urgency, affected persons and operating sector.

Smart Shelter Allocation

Live capacity and occupancy tracking with automated recommendations that prevent over-allocation and streamline check-in / check-out.

Resource & Food Relief

Atomic inventory deduction, low-stock alarms, and fully auditable food and supply distributions to affected populations.

How It Works

From SOS to safety in three steps

1
Report

Citizens raise a rescue request or SOS with location, urgency and number of people affected — no account required.

2
Coordinate

Officers triage, assign teams and shelters; managers track capacity, rations and inventory in real time.

3
Deliver

Volunteers execute assigned tasks while every action is logged for full accountability and impact reporting.
