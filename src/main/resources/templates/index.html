Integrated Disaster Management Ecosystem

Every module in ResQHub is purposely built to reduce response latency, synchronize field forces, and deliver life-saving relief efficiently.

Emergency SOS & Triage

Citizens and ground observers can report urgent distress calls with location, headcount (including children and seniors), and critical medical or trapped flags.

Smart Team Allocation

Algorithmic prioritization matches pending rescue missions with specialized field units (medical, boat, canine, search & rescue) based on vicinity and availability.

Shelter Capacity Network

Real-time occupancy monitoring for emergency shelters, camps, and evacuation zones. Facilitates citizen check-ins, family tracing, and capacity re-routing.

Food & Water Logistics

Tracks food packet preparation, nutritional distribution schedules, and safe drinking water delivery to camps, preventing shortages and distribution bottlenecks.

Medical & Equipment Inventory

Live inventory tracking of essential medical kits, hygiene packs, blankets, boats, and fuel. Automatically highlights low-stock and critical shortages.

Volunteer Mobilization

Enables civic volunteers to register skills, get deployed to verified relief tasks, manage field shifts, and assist camp coordinators with transparent accountability.

About ResQHub

Unifying disaster response networks, safeguarding vulnerable communities, and bridging the critical gap between distress calls and relief action.

The ResQHub Genesis

Born from the Frontlines of Disaster Response

During extreme natural disasters—flash floods, sudden landslides, and coastal cyclones—the critical bottleneck in saving lives is rarely a shortage of human courage; it is information breakdown and siloed communication.

ResQHub was engineered to eliminate this barrier. We serve as a dependable, unified bridge connecting district disaster authorities, first responder battalions (NDRF, SDRF, Fire & Rescue), field medical clinics, relief camp wardens, and grassroots civic volunteers into one synchronized operational picture.

Zero-Delay Triage
Direct dispatch linking stranded families to the closest qualified rescue unit.
Verified Capacities
Continuous ground verification prevents overcrowded camps and resource wastage.
Operated in alignment with National Disaster Management guidelines & community safety frameworks.

Our Mission

To provide an open, lightning-fast coordination platform that reduces disaster response time from hours to minutes, ensuring equitable aid distribution and zero unattended distress calls.

Our Vision

A disaster-resilient society where technology empowers every community to anticipate, endure, and recover swiftly from natural catastrophes with dignity and mutual solidarity.

Community-Driven Solidarity

Technology is only as effective as the community behind it. We provide civic volunteers and camp managers with simple, transparent tools to log needs, distribute rations, and protect the elderly and children.
